Cooking Calendar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information Cooking Calendar ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email us at our support email (coming soon).

The short version

  • Your meals, photos, notes and profile are stored on your device.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • If you use personalized meal ideas, the ingredients and preferences you type are sent to our server and to OpenAI to create suggestions. They are not linked to your name or profile.
  • You can delete your data at any time.

What we collect and why

All profile fields are optional except your display name.

Photos and camera

When you add a meal photo, the app asks for permission to use your photo library or camera. It only uses the photos you pick, and doesn't read the rest of your library.

Personalized meal ideas

When you ask for meal ideas, the app sends the following to our server:

  • the ingredients you list
  • any preferences and allergies you type
  • your cooking time and the number of servings

Our server forwards this to OpenAI to generate suggestions, then sends the ideas back to your device.

  • We don't send your name, email, profile or photos.
  • We ask OpenAI not to store the request (the store: false setting). OpenAI may still keep data for a limited time to monitor for abuse, as described in its own policies: https://openai.com/policies/
  • Our server doesn't keep a copy of your request after it replies.

Please don't type personal or sensitive information into these fields beyond what's needed for cooking.

Backups

When you use Profile → My account → Back up & restore, the app creates one file containing your meals, photos and settings, and opens your phone's share menu. You choose where to keep it, for example iCloud Drive, Google Drive or your email. We never receive or see this file. Anyone who has the file can read what's in it, so keep it somewhere private.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't use advertising, ad tracking or third-party analytics.
  • We don't share your information with anyone, except OpenAI for the meal ideas feature described above, or when the law requires it.

Accounts and cloud backup

Cooking Calendar doesn't currently offer online accounts or cloud backup. Your data stays on your device, so if you delete the app or lose your device, your data may be lost. If we add accounts or backup in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

How long we keep information

Information stored on your device stays there until you delete it or remove the app. Meal idea requests are processed and not kept by us. OpenAI's retention is covered by its own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • See or change your data: you can edit your profile and meals inside the app.
  • Delete your data: delete individual meals in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on the device.
  • Take your data with you: save a backup file at any time from Back up & restore.
  • Photo permissions: you can turn photo and camera access off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), and similar laws elsewhere, you have rights, such as the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, or to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). To use these rights, email our support email (coming soon).

Children

Cooking Calendar is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the meal ideas feature, contact us and we will help.

Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Meal idea requests are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Who is responsible for your information

Cooking Calendar is made by Edward Lee, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for your information under data protection law.
